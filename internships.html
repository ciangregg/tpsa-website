    Academic Internships

    Here is an extensive list of summer research internships for undergraduate students in physics and mathematics curated by members of the TPSA.

    Please note that while we have made every effort to ensure the accuracy of the information provided, some details may be wrong, so please check the official websites of the respective internships for the most accurate info. The TPSA does not take responsibility for any inaccuracies - webmaster.

    Ireland

    DIAS Summer Studentship

    (Theoretical Physics)

    The stipend is €3,000 (€1,500/month). Applications typically open in spring and require a CV, personal statement, and confirmation of eligibility for first-class honors. This is open to students completing their 3rd year at an Irish higher education institution.

    Hamilton Trust

    TCD School of Maths

    Run by the School of Maths in Trinity, this is a six week research project. Students must contact professors about undertaking a project. The professor you must find to supervise the project does not have to be from the School of Maths, but the project topic must have sufficient mathematics content. The research project can be done alone, or in groups of two or three. There is modest financial support, depending on the group size. Information is sent to TCD students via email, usually in early February. This is open to TCD School of maths students in second year or above.

    SURE

    TCD School of Physics

    The Summer Undergraduate Research Experience is a 10-week internship through the School of Physics. Positions have a €300/week stipend and flexible dates. Open to Junior Sophister students in TCD School of Physics considering postgraduate research. Projects are available across experimental, computational, and theoretical physics. Information is sent to eligible TCD students via email, usually in February.

    Laidlaw Scholarship

    TCD Partner

    The Laidlaw program is a two-year programme combining undergraduate research and leadership training. Open to 2nd-year (4-year courses) or 2nd/3rd-year (5-year courses) students from any discipline. Summer 1 involves a six-week self-designed research project (€3,500). Summer 2 involves six-week international leadership placement (€3,500). Students also partake in training days and a conference, and can receive up to €1,900 for research or travel. Around 25 scholarships are awarded annually. Applications due early February, requiring a video, research and leadership proposals, and supervisor support.

    Queen's University Belfast

    Astrophysics Research Centre

    The Astrophysics Research Centre in Belfast offer an eight week summer studentship with support of £2000. The information comes out around April, with the application due at the end of April. This is open to students who have completed at least two years of a degree and have the right to work in the UK.

    Queen's University Belfast

    The Centre for Light-Matter Interactions

    The Centre for Light-Matter Interactions in Belfast offer an eight week summer studentship with support of £2000. The application is due in April/May. This is open to students who have completed at least two years of a degree and have the right to work in the UK.

    Queen's University Belfast

    Mathematical Sciences Research Centre

    The Mathematical Sciences Research Centre ofter a paid internship in varying areas of mathematics. It runs for eight weeks over summer and offers £2000. The right to study in the UK is needed. Applications are due in early summer.

    UCD Mathematics and Statistics

    Students pick from a list of projects provided by the department across their research areas. This is open to any students entering their their final year of studies after the internship. These projects are expected to last a minimum of 8 weeks, starting in early June, with flexible start dates negotiable with individual supervisors. A stipend of €3,000 is provided. Applications are due in early April.

    UCD Physics

    The School of Physics runs Summer Research Studentships in physics and outreach. These are open to all students and run for six weeks with a stipend of €2,400. Applications are due in early April.

    University of Galway Summer Scholarships

    This summer research project from the School of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences is a four week program last year starting June 2nd. This is open to students of any EU university, with preference given to students in their penultimate year. This program offered a bursary of €1300 for a four day work week. Students must find a supervisor willing to supervise, then apply with a short description of the project.

    SETI

    The Breakthrough Listen Undergraduate Research Internship offers an 8-10 week paid position in SETI astrophysics research. There is programs in I-LOFAR in Offaly, Italy and the USA The work is programming-intensive, involving Linux, Docker, and data analysis; experience with Python or similar is highly recommended. Open to undergraduate and Master's students with the right to work in their chose area. Stipend: €600/week. Applications typically close by February 1st and include an interview.

    Britain

    University of Oxford

    The University of Oxford offers a number of internships within its physics department. They all require the right to work in the UK and usually British or Irish citizenship. Students are typically payed the Oxford living wage.

    The Rudolf Peierls Centre for Theoretical Physics

    This program allows students to conduct research in areas of theoretical physics. Applications open in January/February with due date of mid-March. This is open to second year students and above.

    University of Oxford

    Astrophysics

    This program allow for students to do research in data analytics, theoretical astrophysics and instrumentation. The projects run for typically 8 weeks. Open to third year students. The application deadline was is typically in March.

    Atmospheric, Oceanic and Planetary Physics

    These 8 week projects are open to any students with the right to work in the UK. Applications are typically April/May.

    University of Oxford

    Atomic and Laser physics.

    A number of projects are available to apply for, each with their own deadlines, with projects added throughout the year.

    Condensed Matter Physics

    Students are asked to reach out to supervisors directly.

    Cambridge

    Philippa Fawcett Internship Programme

    Cambridge offers the Philippa Fawcett Internship Programme for students who identify as female or non-binary in mathematics who are in second year and above. This is open to students who do not have the right to work in the UK.

    The Cockcroft Institute

    The Cockcroft Institute located between Manchester and Liverpool offers students a minimum 6 week research project across mathematical physics, computer simulation or experimental work. It also offers students a few days training where the full cohort will be based at Daresbury Laboratory to learn about particle accelerator technology. They pay students the national living wage and students must have the right to work in the UK. Students must be entering their third or fourth year of an undergraduate physics or engineering degree after the internship.

    Europe

    CERN

    CERN offers an 8-13 week summer programme (June-September) for Bachelor's or Master's students who have completed at least 6 semesters of studies in physics, engineering, computer science, or mathematics. Students work full-time on a research project in their field and submit a report, with optional poster or public presentation. The programme includes lectures, workshops, tours, and time to explore Switzerland and France. Participants receive ~93 CHF/day, travel reimbursement (~500 CHF from Dublin), full medical insurance, and subsidised accommodation near CERN. Applications close ~31st January. Highly competitive.

    DESY

    DESY (Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron) offers a 7.5-week summer programme for undergraduate students in physics running from from late July to early September in Hamburg or Zeuthen. Projects are available in photon science, elementary particle physics & accelerators and astroparticle physics. Applications take place in January/ February. Students must have completed at least three years of studies.

    RISE

    RISE (Research Internships in Science and Engineering) Germany offers summer research internships for undergraduate students in physics, engineering, and related fields. This is open to undergraduate student studying at a North American, British or Irish University who have completed at least two years of their degree. The three month paid internship takes place at a top German research institutions or university. Applications closes in November.

    LEAPS (Leiden)

    Leiden is an astrophysics programme that runs in the Netherlands. It is open to undergraduate students interested in physics and astronomy who have completed at least two years of a bachelors degree at the time of application and have not yet been accepted onto a PhD program. This is a 10 week summer research project in collaboration with a research scientist from Leiden Observatory or ESA/ESTEC. Applications take place in January/ February

    Munich Center for Quantum Science and Technology Summer Bachelor Program

    This program opens in the Bavarian Alps, before moving to Munich. This program runs from 01 August - 29 August 2026. Applications take place in January/February. There is a €220 participation fee, but all costs, including accommodation and travel are fully covered. This is open to students in an undergraduate degree in physics, mathematics, computer science, electrical engineering, material science, or chemistry in any year of studies.

    Max Planck Institute for Astronomy

    This is a three month position in Heidelberg. applications take place in December/ January. A €1000 per month salary is provided. This is open to bachelor and masters students globally.

    Max Planck Institute for the Physics of Complex Systems

    This is open to students in the last two years of undergraduate study and masters students. Topics include condensed matter and quantum information theory. Applications take place in November. The program runs for a minimum of two months in Dresden.

    Rest of the World

    ASIAA (Taipei)

    The Academia Sinica Institute of Astronomy and Astrophysics (ASIAA) in Taipei offers a summer internship programme for undergraduate students interested in astrophysics and astronomy. Participants conduct original research in observational, theoretical, or laboratory-based projects under the supervision of a professional astronomer at ASIAA. This is open to graduate and undergraduate students in mathematical, physical, or computational sciences, or engineering, reference will be given to those entering their 3rd or 4th year of undergraduate studies or beginning a Master's program with preference given to those entering their 3rd or 4th year of undergraduate studies or beginning a Master's program. Applications take place in February/March

    UTRIP (Tokyo)

    This is a six week program in Tokyo open to students from outside Japan. Open to second years and above who are returning to a bachelors studies after the program. A field trip to a historical or natural site in Japan, and social and cultural events including language classes are also part of the program.

    Perimeter Institute

    The Perimeter Scholars International (PSI) in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada offer a two week undergraduate summer school.They also offer select students positions on a paid internship program. Applications must intend on applying toa graduate program and have knowledge of classical mechanics and quantum mechanics before the start of the program. Applications take place in January.

    Sao Carlos Institute of Physics Brazil

    The program is multidisciplinary in nature, offering research projects across a broad range of topics, from Theoretical Physics to Applied Atomic Physics and Biophysics. The program will be fully funded and IFSC will provide housing, airfare and a monthly allowance for all selected candidates. This program is aimed at students who are close to completing their undergraduate studies. Deadlines vary yearly.

    SURF Caltech

    This is a ten week program which takes place on the Caltech campus. A Caltech faculty mentor must agree to support your application, help you to develop and define a research project, and assist you in the writing of the research proposal. Applications typically take place in February/ March Applications need US citizenship for some projects.

    Simons Foundation

    The Simons Foundation offers a number of internship programs from computational programs in quantum physics and astrophysics, writing for Quanta Magazine, and the Simons-NSBP Scholars Program, each with their own deadline. Most in person programs take place in New York.
