Qiskit Fall Fest 2026

Qiskit Fall Fest is a student-led, worldwide series of events that brings people together to explore and learn about quantum computing. Sign up to our Quantum Industry Challenge Day on 28th October below:

Events

This year's Fall Fest has a range of events, from Qiskit workshops and academic talks to a quantum innovation challenge and industry panel. This is open to TPSA members, students from TCD, UCD and any other university. We also welcome interested members of the public. Registration for the Quantum Innovation Challenge on the 5th October. Registration is not required for the other events. Details about each event will also be send to the TPSA mailing list.

Quantum Industry Panel Session

Location: Moore Auditorium, UCD O'Brien Center for Science Date: Thursday 8th October, 6–8pm

This event will bring together speakers from the regional quantum academic and industrial ecosystems.

The panel discussion will explore where the field of quantum technology currently stands, how it could evolve in the near future, and where students can get involved in the quantum industry or through further academic study.
Panel Host: Conor Ryan - PhD Student in the School of Physics at Trinity College Dublin.
Panelists: Prof Steve Cambell: UCD, Dr Niall McEvoy: Research Ireland, Sean Casey: IBM, Niall Murphy: Equal1

Qiskit Workshop

Location: Synge Lecture Theatre, Hamilton Building, TCD
Date: Tuesday 13th October, 6–8pm

Qiskit is a Python tool for quantum computing. This workshop will introduce the fundamentals of quantum computing and teach you how to write your own programs in Qiskit. No prior knowledge of quantum computing is required.

The session will be led by PhD student Conor Ryan and will provide a hands-on introduction to quantum computing and Qiskit.

Academic Talks

We will also be hosting a number of talks throughout the Fall Fest period from academics at both University College Dublin and Trinity College Dublin.

These talks will provide an opportunity to learn about current research in quantum computing and related areas directly from researchers working in the field.

Prof Pieter Claeys (School of Physics TCD) will give a talk on the 19th October at 6pm in the Joly Lecture Theatre, Hamilton Building, TCD.
Title: From Quantum Computers to Quantum Dynamics and Back
Abstract: In recent years, experimental advances in quantum computing have gone hand in hand with theoretical advances in our understanding of the dynamical behaviour of quantum systems. Quantum computing platforms present a natural playground for the experimental study of quantum dynamics, leading to the direct observation of a rich variety of dynamical behaviours. Conversely, many quantum algorithms are closely tied to fundamental dynamical notions of ergodicity and chaos. In this talk, I will use quantum circuits as minimal models of quantum dynamics to explore this two-way connection: how quantum computers can serve as laboratories for quantum dynamics, and how insights from quantum dynamics have in turn motivated a new generation of quantum experiments.
The talk will be followed by a social event, where you can get to know other students participating in Qiskit Fall Fest events.

Quantum Innovation Challenge

Location: TCD
Date: 28th October, 10-5pm

We will be hosting a day-long Quantum Innovation Challenge, where participants will use their knowledge of quantum mechanics and quantum computing to propose applications of quantum technology in an unrelated field.

Participants can sign up as a team or create a team on the day. Each team will then be randomly assigned a non-physics field that could benefit from quantum technology, such as finance, healthcare or meteorology.

Over the course of the day, teams will design a quantum-based solution to a topical problem in their assigned field before presenting their ideas to fellow participants and invited experts from academia and industry.
