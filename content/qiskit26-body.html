Qiskit Fall Fest 2026

Qiskit Fall Fest is a student-led, worldwide series of events that brings people together to explore and learn about quantum computing.

Events

This year's Fall Fest has a range of events, from Qiskit workshops and academic talks to a quantum innovation challenge and industry panel. This is open to TPSA members, students from TCD, UCD and any other university. We also welcome interested members of the public. Some events will require registration, check back here or our instagram tpsa_ireland for updates!

Quantum Industry Panel Session

Location: UCD

This event will bring together speakers from the regional quantum academic and industrial ecosystems.

The panel discussion will explore where the field of quantum technology currently stands, how it could evolve in the near future, and where students can get involved in the quantum industry or through further academic study.

Qiskit Workshop

Location: Synge Lecture Theatre, Hamilton Building, TCD
Date: Tuesday 13th October, 6–8pm

Qiskit is a Python tool for quantum computing. This workshop will introduce the fundamentals of quantum computing and teach you how to write your own programs in Qiskit.

The session will be led by PhD student Conor Ryan and will provide a hands-on introduction to quantum computing and Qiskit.

The workshop will be followed by a social event, where you can get to know other students participating in Qiskit Fall Fest events.

Quantum Innovation Challenge

Location: TCD
Date: Week of 26th–30th October

We will be hosting a day-long Quantum Innovation Challenge, where participants will use their knowledge of quantum mechanics and quantum computing to propose applications of quantum technology in an unrelated field.

Participants can sign up as a team or create a team on the day. Each team will then be randomly assigned a non-physics field that could benefit from quantum technology, such as finance, healthcare or meteorology.

Over the course of the day, teams will design a quantum-based solution to a topical problem in their assigned field before presenting their ideas to fellow participants and invited experts from academia and industry.

Sign-up details will be available here soon.

Poster Session

Location: UCD
Date: Mid-November

The Qiskit Fall Fest poster session will be open to both students and early-career academics.

This will be an opportunity to share research related to quantum computing with students, academics and industry professionals, while discovering the work being carried out across the community.

Academic Talks

We will also be hosting a number of talks throughout the Fall Fest period from academics at both University College Dublin and Trinity College Dublin.

These talks will provide an opportunity to learn about current research in quantum computing and related areas directly from researchers working in the field.

More details about the speakers and talks will be announced throughout Fall Fest.
